# Real Paintings

Hang your own pictures in your player-owned house. Your dog, your wedding photo, your
first fire cape screenshot: any image on your computer can replace the paintings and maps
in your quest hall.

Pictures are stored and shown **only on your computer**. Nothing is uploaded, and other
players still see the original paintings.

## How to use

There are three ways to set a picture. Use whichever is easiest:

1. **Right-click the painting** in your house and choose **Set picture**, then pick an image file.
2. **Use the sidebar panel** (the framed-picture icon). Click **Choose...** next to any painting.
3. **Drop files into the image folder.** Click **Open image folder** in the panel and copy
   images in, named after the painting they replace:

| Painting                 | File name           |
|--------------------------|---------------------|
| King Arthur portrait     | `arthur.png`        |
| Elena portrait           | `elena.png`         |
| Giant dwarf portrait     | `giant-dwarf.png`   |
| Miscellanians portrait   | `miscellanians.png` |
| Lumbridge landscape      | `lumbridge.png`     |
| The Desert landscape     | `desert.png`        |
| Morytania landscape      | `morytania.png`     |
| Karamja landscape        | `karamja.png`       |
| Isafdar landscape        | `isafdar.png`       |
| Small map                | `small-map.png`     |
| Medium map               | `medium-map.png`    |
| Large map                | `large-map.png`     |

`.png`, `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.gif` and `.bmp` all work. The folder is checked every few
seconds, so new or edited files appear without restarting.

To go back to the original painting, right-click it and choose **Clear picture**, click
**Clear** in the panel, or delete the file.

## Settings

- **Image fit**: *Crop to fill* (default) trims the picture to the painting's shape.
  *Fit inside* shows the whole picture with black bars. *Stretch* distorts it to fill.
- **Frame size**: how much of the original painting's border stays visible as a frame.
- **Brightness**: darkens the picture so it matches the room's lighting.
- **Opacity**: lets the original painting show through.
- **Right-click options**: shows or hides the *Set picture* / *Clear picture* menu options.

## Known limitations

- The picture is drawn as an overlay on top of the game view, so it shows in front of
  anything that walks or stands between the camera and the painting.
- Pictures apply to that painting type in any house you visit, not only your own.
